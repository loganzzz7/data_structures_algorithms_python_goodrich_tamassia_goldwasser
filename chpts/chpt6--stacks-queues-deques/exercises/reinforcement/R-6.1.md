## What values are returned during the following series of stack operations, if executed upon an initially empty stack?

**Operations**: `push(5), push(3), pop(), push(2), push(8), pop(), pop(), push(9), push(1), pop(), push(7), push(6), pop(), pop(), push(4), pop(), pop()`

- Stack = `LIFO`--`Last-in, First-out`
    ```text
    Stack:
    5, 3

    pop() -> 3
    
    Stack:
    5, 2, 8

    pop() -> 8, 2

    Stack:
    5, 9, 1

    pop() -> 1

    Stack:
    5, 9, 7, 6

    pop() -> 6, 7

    Stack:
    5, 9, 4

    pop() -> 4, 9

    Final Stack: 5
    ```
